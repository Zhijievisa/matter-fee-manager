Matter Fee Manager — Help & Support

Staged fee schedules for fixed-fee work, inside Trello.

Getting started

  1. Open a card. Click the Power-Ups (rocket) button in the bar below the card, then Fee schedule.
  2. Choose New blank fee schedule or a template. A "Staged fee schedule" section appears on the card.
  3. Enter each stage's name and amount, then click Save. Optionally enter the contract total; you are warned if the stages do not add up to it.

Moving a stage along

  • Done · to invoice: the work for this stage is finished. The card shows a yellow badge.
  • Invoiced · unpaid: you have billed it. Enter the invoice number. The due date is filled in from your payment terms and can be changed. Enter a part payment in Received if the client pays some of it.
  • Paid: paid in full. The paid date defaults to today.

Once the due date passes, the badge turns red and shows the number of days overdue.

Seeing everything that is owed

Click Receivables at the top of the board (it may be inside the board's Power-Ups menu). It lists every stage that needs invoicing or chasing, most urgent first, and totals for the whole board. Export CSV gives you the full list for a spreadsheet.

Settings

Board menu → Power-Ups → Matter Fee Manager → Settings: language (English or 简体中文), currency symbol, default payment terms, and stage templates.

Frequently asked questions

Who can see the fee figures? Board members with edit access. Other viewers do not see badges or the schedule. Note that Trello stores Power-Up data so that anyone able to view the board can technically read it, so do not use this on a public board for confidential figures.

Does it send reminders? No. A Power-Up only runs while a board is open in a browser. Use the badges and the Receivables overview.

Two of us edited the same card. If someone else saves while you are editing, you are asked which version to keep. Nothing is overwritten silently.

"Exceeds Trello's storage limit". Trello allows about 4,000 characters of Power-Up data per card, which is roughly 20 stages. Shorten stage names or split the matter across cards.

Is this an accounting system? No. Figures are entered by hand for tracking and are not accounting, tax or trust account records.
