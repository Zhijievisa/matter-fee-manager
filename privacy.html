Matter Fee Manager — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Matter Fee Manager is a Power-Up for Trello published by ZHIJIEVISA PTY LTD (ABN 89 167 335 257), Suite 3, Level 6, 68 Grenfell St, Adelaide ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Summary

  • Everything you enter is stored in your own Trello board, using Trello's Power-Up data storage.
  • We do not run a server for this Power-Up. We do not receive, see, copy or store your board data.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking are used by the Power-Up.

What the Power-Up stores, and where

When you create a fee schedule, the Power-Up saves the following on the Trello card it belongs to: stage names, amounts, statuses, amounts received, invoice numbers and the dates you set (done, invoiced, due, paid). Board-level settings (language, currency symbol, payment terms and stage templates) are saved on the Trello board.

This information is held by Trello (Atlassian) as part of your board, under your agreement with Atlassian and the Atlassian Privacy Policy. It is stored with Trello's "shared" visibility, which means anyone who can view the board in Trello can read it, including through Trello's API. Do not use the Power-Up on a public board if the fee information is confidential.

What we receive

Nothing from your boards. The Power-Up runs entirely in your browser and talks only to Trello.

If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems. We keep support correspondence only for as long as we need it to handle your request and for our ordinary business records, and we will delete it earlier if you ask us to, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Third parties involved in delivering the Power-Up

  • Trello (Atlassian) stores your data and loads the Power-Up inside Trello. The Power-Up also loads Trello's Power-Up client library from Trello's servers.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Power-Up's program files (HTML, JavaScript, CSS). When your browser downloads these files, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. No board data is sent to GitHub.

Deleting your data

You can delete a card's fee schedule at any time from the Power-Up's menu on that card ("Delete this card's fee schedule"). Deleting or archiving a card, or removing the Power-Up's data when you disable it, is handled by Trello. Because we hold none of your board data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Not an accounting or trust record

Figures in the Power-Up are entered by hand for tracking purposes. They are not accounting, tax or trust account records.
